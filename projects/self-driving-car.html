Self-driving Power Wheels jeep

Retrofitted a Power Wheels jeep with better power management, a Raspberry Pi 5, LIDAR, RTK GPS, and two OAK-D cameras for autonomous navigation. Developed with Prof. Jack Silberman as an achievable project for high school engineering programs.

Role
Lead designer, electronics fabrication
Compute
Raspberry Pi 5
Sensors
LIDAR, RTK GPS, 2× OAK-D
Drive
VESC motor controllers on CAN

Electronics retrofit

My role was planning and fabricating the electronics: soldering new silicone wiring and connectors to each VESC and motor, and crimping the CAN bus.
